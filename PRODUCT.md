# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Retail CFD traders (primarily Thai, with English/Thai bilingual support) who journal and analyze their own trading performance. They review this app during active trading sessions and post-session reviews — often in low-light environments, under pressure, needing fast data reads.

## Product Purpose

A personal trading journal and analytics platform. Traders log trades, track performance metrics, review patterns, and build discipline. Success = a trader who can open the dashboard and immediately understand where they stand.

## Brand Personality

Precise, elevated, calm. The tool should feel like a serious instrument — not a game, not a startup. Confidence without aggression. Stripe meets TradingView.

## Anti-references

- Crypto exchange dashboards: neon gradients, information overload, anxiety-inducing UI
- Generic SaaS dashboards: cream backgrounds, rounded blob shapes, identical icon-grid cards
- Bloomberg Terminal: too dense and monochrome — readability matters

## Design Principles

1. **Data first** — every UI element earns its place by helping the trader read or enter data faster
2. **Earned calm** — whitespace is a tool, not decoration; density is used only when data density requires it
3. **Restrained precision** — one accent color, used for state and action only, never decoration
4. **Bilingual by design** — Thai and English text must both look correct at every size
5. **Session-aware** — dark mode is a first-class citizen, not an afterthought

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast)
- Reduced motion respected
- Both Thai and English must read cleanly at all type sizes
